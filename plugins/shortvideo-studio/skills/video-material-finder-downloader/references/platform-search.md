# Source search routing

Choose sources from the script beat's visual job. Use both Chinese and English keyword variants for international people, institutions, research, products, and events. Search the smallest useful set; do not query every platform for every beat.

## Routing table

| Visual job | Search first | Escalate when needed |
|---|---|---|
| Named Chinese event, company, person, or product | Official website/media center, Weibo, Douyin, Xiaohongshu, Bilibili | Direct no-fee creator permission or generated visual |
| Named international event, company, person, or product | Official website/media center, YouTube, X, TikTok, Reddit | Direct no-fee creator permission or generated visual |
| Eyewitness, disaster, accident, viral or caught-on-camera footage | Original social post | Direct no-fee creator permission or generated visual |
| Generic office, city, factory, lab, logistics, devices, or lifestyle B-roll | Pexels, Pixabay, Mixkit Free License | Generated visual |
| Historical event or archival context | Wikimedia Commons, Internet Archive, Prelinger Archives | Official institution archive or generated explanation visual |
| Official launch, speech, report, institution, or product demo | Organization newsroom, press kit, investor relations, government or university media page | Verified official social channel |

## Chinese social video

- **Weibo:** Use Weibo search, its video tab, an available Weibo search tool, or a signed-in browser. Combine the subject with `现场`, `发布会`, `官方通报`, `采访`, `演讲`, or `原视频`. Retain the exact post and account; record original versus repost.
- **Douyin:** Search the event, person, organization, location, and `现场`/`官方` variants. Prefer verified official accounts and the earliest plausible recorder. Expand share links to the public post when possible; expect login, anti-bot, watermark, or extractor failure.
- **Xiaohongshu:** Use it for consumer scenes, local events, store openings, travel, exhibitions, and first-person vertical footage. Prefer the original note and author; avoid using a repost or commercial seeding post as event evidence. Expect login or manual browser download.
- **Bilibili:** Prefer official accounts, universities, conferences, interviews, documentaries, full events, and original factual uploads. Retain canonical `https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV.../` pages and inspect repost labels, multi-part selection, watermarks, danmaku, burned-in subtitles, and “未经作者授权，禁止转载”.

## Global social video

- **YouTube:** Search official channels, full talks, interviews, universities, conferences, company demonstrations, raw footage, and original creators. Return canonical watch links and inspect the selected license plus optional or burned-in subtitles.
- **X:** Use for breaking news, journalists, institutions, companies, public officials, eyewitnesses, and event chronology. Retain the exact status, author, date, and whether the attached media is original or quoted from another account.
- **TikTok:** Use for vertical eyewitness footage, consumer behavior, local scenes, culture, and creator-originated clips. Prefer the earliest original post and screen watermarks, music rights, captions, and repost chains.
- **Reddit:** Use for community-sourced footage, original upload context, location clues, and discovery of the recorder. Retain the exact post plus any credited source. Treat cross-posts and embedded third-party media as leads, not proof of ownership.

## Official websites and media centers

- Search company newsrooms, press kits, investor-relations pages, government agencies, universities, research labs, conferences, museums, and international organizations before ordinary reposts.
- Useful queries include `<name> newsroom video`, `<product> press kit`, `<event> media center`, `<person> full speech`, and `<organization> official footage`.
- Retain the page containing the asset and its usage terms. If the site offers a visible official download, label it `official-download`; otherwise retain it as a source link and record the permission requirement.

## Stock footage

- Use **Pexels**, **Pixabay**, and only items under the **Mixkit Free License**. Retain the detail page and current item license. Prefer clean B-roll without visible logos or burned-in text.
- Do not search paid stock sites, subscription libraries, paid packs, or paid individual assets.
- Use stock for generic concepts such as coding, laboratories, campuses, offices, servers, chips, autonomous driving, teamwork, and abstract AI visuals. Never use generic stock as evidence of a named person or specific event.

## Historical and open archives

- Search **Wikimedia Commons**, **Internet Archive**, and **Prelinger Archives** for public-domain or openly licensed historical material.
- Verify the license on the individual file or item page; a repository being open does not make every hosted file reusable.
- Retain creator, source institution, license, attribution text, and any jurisdiction or personality-right caveat.
- Do not escalate to any paid archive. If suitable open or official archival footage is unavailable, report the gap and use an existing generated explanation visual when appropriate.

## Shortlist coverage

- For each major script beat, shortlist the strongest 1–3 candidates from the routed source classes.
- Record source class, platform, exact page, title, uploader, publish date, suggested range, orientation, overlay/subtitle note, rights note, and delivery status.
- Prefer a smaller set of clearly relevant clips over repetitive results, reviews, reactions, unverifiable reposts, or watermarked previews.
