# Style Layer

## Purpose

This file is the always-on style gate for the short-video workflow. Read it first and keep it active through topic choice, structure design, titles, and final script writing.

## Voice

- Sound like a sharp Chinese business/consumer observer explaining a pattern to one real person.
- Stay conversational and speakable, but do not flatten the thinking into empty口语 filler.
- Sound confident and clear, not preachy, performative, or over-produced.
- Prefer judgment earned by examples and mechanism over abstract slogans.

## Hook Rules

- Open with a gold 5-second hook that can immediately pull the viewer in.
- The hook may be a phenomenon,反差, direct question, sharp judgment, or any other fast-entry move that creates immediate attention; it does not need to begin with a vivid scene every time.
- Make the tension visible within the first few lines.
- Let the viewer quickly understand: `今天这条到底要解释什么？`
- Do not begin with slow background, generic scene-setting, or self-conscious grand statements.

## Structure Rhythm

- Default progression: `现象 -> 解释机制 -> 举例/理论补强 -> 收束判断`.
- Move from visible case to underlying logic fast; do not linger on recap.
- Let each paragraph or beat do one job only.
- Treat the ending as a perspective upgrade, not a repetition of the opening.

## Language Preferences

- Use口语化 Chinese that can be spoken naturally at full speed.
- Prefer crisp, complete sentences over overcompressed fragments.
- Use concepts or labels only when they genuinely clarify the pattern.
- Allow one memorable phrase or coined concept when it gives the script a spine.
- Use theory sparingly and translate it into plain language immediately.

## Anti-Drift Rules

- Do not write in公众号长文 density. The script should feel speakable, not essay-like.
- Do not turn the script into a bullet memo or consulting framework.
- Do not rely on repeated `不是……而是……`, `某种意义上`, `值得注意的是`, `不可否认`, or similar stock pivots.
- Do not sound like a励志博主 or moral lecturer.
- Do not stack examples without cashing them out into one clear judgment.

## Title Preferences

- Favor question-led, contradiction-led, or mechanism-led titles.
- Prefer titles that already imply the explanation angle, not just the event.
- Keep titles sharp and传播友好, but avoid tabloid exaggeration.
- Five candidates should vary across question, judgment, contrast, scene, and concept framing.

## Ending Preferences

- End in the way that best serves the script's core argument.
- A strong ending can upgrade the frame, land on a bigger消费/传播机制, or simply drive home the main judgment with force and clarity.
- Do not force every script into a bigger-systems ending if the topic works better with a direct, sharper close.
- Keep the last lines short enough to land cleanly in spoken form.

## Formatting Preference

- Default to paragraph-led delivery instead of stacking many very short standalone lines.
- Preserve口播节奏 through sentence rhythm and paragraph breaks, not through excessive line-by-line fragmentation.
- Use explicit numbered layers such as `第一点` `第二点` `第三点` when they improve listener comprehension.

Stable voice signals found across the corpus:

- Start with a vivid, familiar, or slightly魔幻的现象, then flip fast into the real question.
- Treat one observable detail as the entry point to a larger business, social, or platform logic.
- Use direct, spoken transitions that sound like a person talking, not like an essay changing sections.

Structural habits found in the corpus:

- Titles often use `为什么...？` or a contradiction that already contains tension.

- The last paragraph often zooms out and tells the viewer what this case really says about the times, the market, or human behavior.

## 2026-04-20 User Preference Addendum

- For brand, consumer, platform, and business-phenomenon topics, translate analysis into mass emotion, identity, and trust language rather than staying in earnings-call vocabulary.
- In the first 5 seconds, prefer a high-tension entry such as collapse, fall-from-grace, belief-breaking, or sudden loss of appeal when the facts can support that intensity.
- Default opening rhythm: hit the conflict first, then reveal the fact pattern, then explain why it matters.
- Each reason block should usually contain one sharp judgment, one concrete example or short data point, and one plain-language interpretation.
- Use memorable labels and spoken metaphors when they improve recall, especially in hook lines, transitions, and the ending.
- Treat data as proof that locks in the judgment, not as the main performance. Quote only the few numbers that matter.
- When writing about premium brands, consider whether the deeper story is loss of freshness, identity drift, trust erosion, or consumers switching from admiration to scrutiny.
- Endings can carry more verdict and bite than a typical explainer, as long as they still match the evidence.
- For legally, medically, or financially sensitive claims, keep a dual-track instinct: strong spoken copy in the script, paired with a safer backup phrasing note when needed.

- Topic selection should favor explainable tension over surface heat. Strong recent samples often ask why a familiar thing is suddenly `不香了`, `卖不动了`, `退出中国`, `越来越少`, `又火了`, or being `重新审问`.

- For consumer and brand topics, move quickly from event to human motive: anxiety, trust, identity, freshness, social safety, price anchors, fear of being tricked, fear of brands running away, or the desire to feel that one has not been treated like a sucker.
- A strong opening can use a familiar daily question, then flip it into the real mechanism: `你多久没有...`, `你有没有发现...`, `现在最魔幻的是...`, `为什么...越来越...`, or `那个曾经...的品牌，现在...`.
- Good hooks often combine one hard fact with one human translation. Do not just say revenue fell; say what consumer belief collapsed, what use case disappeared, or what old promise no longer convinces people.
- The default body rhythm should be `现象/数据 -> 第一层原因 -> 第二层原因 -> 第三层原因 -> 表面看/深层看收束`. Each reason should include a concrete signal, a business mechanism, and a plain spoken interpretation.
- Prefer mechanisms that explain redistribution of power: consumers move from仰视 to审问, brands move from心智红利 to信任考试, platforms move from增长机器 to责任主体, AI moves from工具兴奋 to岗位重估.

- Endings should usually land on a verdict with a second-layer meaning: this is not only a brand problem, but a change in consumer trust, lifestyle, public space, work value, or industry power structure.
- For topic cards, mark as higher priority when the case can be explained through one of these frames: `消费者重新审问`, `信任资产松动`, `免费福利被收紧`, `焦虑被商业化`, `旧场景被手机/外卖/AI 替代`, `品牌光环不再值钱`.
- For risky claims involving health, law, lawsuits, public figures, financial performance, or AI layoffs, keep the punchy script instinct but verify current facts and add conservative phrasing notes.
